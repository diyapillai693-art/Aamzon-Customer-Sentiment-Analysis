# Aamzon-Customer-Sentiment-Analysis
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 10,000 customer reviews to explore sentiment, ratings, product performance, and customer satisfaction.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer sentiment and rating distribution.
* Identify products with higher negative feedback.
* Track sentiment trends over time.
* Compare sentiment across categories, brands, and verified purchases.
* Generate insights to support product improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and cleaning
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and visualizations
* **DAX** – KPI calculations and analytical measures

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

* Total Reviews, Total Products, Average Rating, and Sentiment KPIs
* Sentiment Distribution and Reviews by Rating
* Sentiment by Category and Brand
* Monthly Sentiment and Sentiment Score Trends
* Negative Review Percentage by Product
* Helpful Votes vs. Sentiment
* Verified vs. Non-Verified Purchase Analysis
* Rating vs. Sentiment Matrix
* Interactive slicers for products, categories, brands, ratings, and sentiment

## 💡 Key Insights

* Positive reviews represent the largest share of feedback.
* Customer ratings generally align with sentiment classification.
* Negative feedback varies across products and categories.
* Review trends and product-level analysis highlight potential improvement areas.
